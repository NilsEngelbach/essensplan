- **[P0] Grocery list fails in store:** in-memory ticks (grocery-list/page.tsx:41), per-recipe not merged, no aisles, mandatory select step, div checkboxes. Fix: merged, aisle-grouped default list; saved, offline check state; big rows; selection in a sheet. Commands: shape einkaufsliste → harden, adapt.
- **[P1] AI import hidden; preview read-only:** only a grey button on /recipes; after saving you don't land on the recipe. Fix: smart capture field as the entry point of "Neues Rezept"; editable result. Commands: shape ki-import → onboard, distill.
